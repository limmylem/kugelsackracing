Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-06

A starting draft written with the game. The game's owner should review it (ideally with legal advice) before the game is opened to the public.

What's kept

  • Your account: your email address, display name, a scrambled form of your password (never the password itself), your role, when you joined, and when you accepted these terms. Your date of birth is only checked when you sign up, never kept.
  • Signing in: your sessions, each with the internet address and browser it came from, so you can sign out everywhere and so abuse can be stopped. If you sign in with Google or Discord, the account id and profile name they give us.
  • Playing: the results you hand in (with the run's recording, as evidence for the leaderboards), your track records, and the race replays you keep (your newest 50, and your records').
  • In your browser: your garage and progress (kept in this browser, tied to your account), your settings, and two cookies the game needs: one keeps you signed in, one protects your requests from other sites. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Admins' log: anything an admin does to an account (a role change, a suspension) is logged with the reason.

Who sees it

Other players see your display name, your times and places on leaderboards, and replays shared with them. The game's admins can see your account to help you or to stop abuse. The game runs on hosting providers (its server and its database) and sends email through an email provider; they keep the data only to run the game. Errors in the game are reported to an error-tracking service without your name, email or anything you typed. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Your choices

  • A copy: download everything the server keeps about you from the account page.
  • Deleting: delete your account from the account page: your account, results, records and replays are deleted at once (backups of the database roll off within 30 days). World content you made as an editor stays in the game, no longer linked to you.
  • Guests: a guest account is deleted when it's made into a full account (its progress moves across).

Contact

Questions about your data: contact the game's owner.
