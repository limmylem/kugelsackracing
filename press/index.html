In a sentence

Ogni Strada is a free driving game in the browser where you race on real roads, in real cities built from open map data.

In a paragraph

Ogni Strada turns real places into a driving game you play in a web browser. San Francisco's hills, Adelaide's grid, Monaco's harbour, Shibuya's crossings and the hairpins of the Stelvio Pass are all built from OpenStreetMap and open elevation data. The cars have real physics: grip, weight transfer, brake fade and damage you fix in the garage. Build and tune your car, take on quests and street races against rivals, learn car control in the driving school, and meet friends in free roam. It's free, with no adverts and nothing to buy, and made to be played by everyone, with full control remapping, touch controls, colour-blind palettes, reduced motion and the important sounds shown on screen.

Features

  • Seven real regions: San Francisco, Adelaide, Monaco, Tokyo (Shibuya), the Stelvio Pass, Munich and Milton Keynes.
  • Driving physics with tyre grip, weight transfer, brake fade, and dents and broken parts that you repair.
  • A garage with parts to buy and fit, tuning, paint and a dyno. Every car is the game's own design: no real makes or brands.
  • Quests, sprints, time trials and street races against rivals; generated tracks you share by code; online races.
  • A driving school with seven lessons and medals, and a guided first drive.
  • Photo mode with a free camera, depth of field, filters and time of day.
  • Music composed by the game itself, live, so it's safe for streams and videos.
  • Accessibility: remappable controls with hold or toggle, touch controls, text size, high contrast, colour-blind palettes, reduced motion and flashing, sounds shown on screen, keyboard and screen-reader menus.
